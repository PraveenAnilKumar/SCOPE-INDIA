SCOPE INDIA, your career partner!

One of India's best Training destinations for Advanced Data Science and Artificial Intelligence, Advanced Data Science with Gen AI, Advanced Data Analytics, Software Development, DevOps and Cloud Computing courses with 19 years of Industrial experience. Over 1,000 students find their dream careers each year, and we have assisted more than 16,000 students so far.

SCOPE INDIA - A Professional Education Centre in Kerala and Tamil Nadu from Suffix E Solutions, an IT company - is a trusted training institute dedicated to helping students build successful careers in the IT industry through practical learning and industry-focused training. With 19 years of experience in professional education, SCOPE INDIA has become a preferred choice for students who want to gain real-world technical skills and enhance their career opportunities. SCOPE INDIA always focuses on a job-oriented curriculum that helps students adapt to new technologies.

At SCOPE INDIA, learning is designed to be practical and career-oriented. The courses are structured to help students understand how real IT companies work, giving them the confidence and skills needed to start their careers successfully. Instead of focusing only on theory, students get opportunities to work on live projects and internship-based training that improve their technical knowledge and hands-on experience. SCOPE INDIA provides courses for Advanced Data Science and Artificial Intelligence, Advanced Data Science with Gen AI, Advanced Data Analytics, Python Full Stack, Java Full Stack, .Net Full Stack, PHP Full Stack, Power BI with excel advanced, Advanced MERN Full Stack, MEAN Full Stack, Software Testing Manual and Automation, Playwright, Cloud Computing (AWS / Azure), Server Administration (Linux-RHCE), Networking (CCNA), DevOps (Docker, Kubernetes, Jenkins- CI/CD) Mobile App Development in Flutter, and Advanced Digital Marketing.

One of the biggest advantages of learning at SCOPE INDIA is the 100% placement support provided to students who successfully complete their training programs. From resume preparation and interview guidance to placement assistance, students receive complete support throughout their career journey.

SCOPE INDIA provides On the Job Training (OJT) for students who have completed their course. During this period, they work on real-time and commercial projects from Suffix E Solutions - an IT company. This experience makes students 100% confident in understanding how real projects start and finish, including the end-to-end project delivery process.

Students also gain valuable project experience during their internship period, helping them improve problem-solving abilities, teamwork, and practical implementation skills. This real-time exposure prepares them to work confidently in professional environments from the very beginning of their careers.

What truly makes SCOPE INDIA different is the continuous support offered even after placement. The institution believes in building long-term relationships with students by providing lifelong career guidance, professional assistance, and skill support whenever needed.

With branches in Thiruvananthapuram, Kochi, Nagercoil, and Technopark, SCOPE INDIA continues to guide aspiring professionals toward a brighter future through quality education, practical training, and dedicated career support.

Our Story

Founded in 2007

With over two decades of experience creating extensive internet systems, Suffix E Solutions is a top provider of networking and software services. By concentrating on our clients' primary needs, we provide dependable and useful solutions that are suited to their specifications. From documentation and framework design to coding, testing, and continuing maintenance, we have experience with every stage of the development lifecycle. Our knowledgeable engineers, who have 18 years of experience in a variety of fields, offer detailed direction at every stage of the development process. Our vast knowledge base enables us to provide our clients with accurate and efficient solutions.

An ISO 9001:2015 Certified Company

Evolution

SCOPE INDIA was launched in 2015 by Suffix E Solutions to address the growing demand for industry-relevant IT training under proficient working professionals. What began as a mission to bridge the gap between education and employment has grown into one of the most recognized names in tech education.

Growth

SCOPE INDIA began its journey in Trivandrum in 2015 and has steadily grown into a multi-location training powerhouse. In 2019, we expanded to Kochi to support more aspiring professionals. In 2021, the Nagercoil branch was proudly launched, and in 2023, we launched our Technopark branch - bringing our high-quality training even closer to the heart of Kerala's IT hub. Each branch reflects our commitment to making world-class IT education accessible to more people.
